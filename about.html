Biography

Mira Ceti

Born and raised in Paris, Mira started her career at the age of 12, singing at that time in the prestigious yet innovative Chœur d’enfants Sotto Voce. She spent 8 years performing and touring as a chorister, from operas to musicals, acquiring a solid basis and becoming addicted to the stage.

After that experience, she specialized in traditional music, studying the vocal techniques of various cultures around Europe. Leaving whenever she could for trips that took her from Jerusalem to Peru, she observed the role of voice and sound in the sacred rites of traditional societies.

These diverse influences led her to develop a musical language that draws on traditional songs from different cultures, highlighting shared essences and developing “syncretic rites.” Working primarily around the notion of the sacred, she seeks to transmit the memory of ancestral ways of life punctuated by the seasons and associated festivals.

Mira Ceti performs on stage accompanied by her musical partner n.hir, an electronic and acoustic music producer and founder of the experimental label Alcôve.

A member of several projects, she created the show “Elzear” with the equestrian company “Les chevaux célestes.” In 2019, she joined Heilung as a singer and has been touring with the band ever since.

She released her debut album, Himest Mesi, in 2023.
